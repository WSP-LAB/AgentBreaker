  • Trailblazers of geometric abstraction

    The University of Michigan Museum of Art brings “Both Sides of the Line: Carmen Herrera & Leon Polk Smith,” an exhibition uniting two of the 20th century’s most visionary abstract artists, to Ann Arbor.

    Learn more about this exhibition

  • World Solar Challenge

    Racing under reduced winter sunlight for the first time, the University of Michigan solar car was the leading North American car during the 2025 Bridgestone World Solar Challenge. They crossed the finish line in Adelaide, Australia in the afternoon, marking the end of their four-day endurance race across 1,800 miles of the Australian Outback.

    Learn more about the race
